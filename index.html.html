How it works

1
He verifies himself. He confirms his identity against a government ID and attests to a few facts before anything is searched.
2
A check runs across states. Public records in your region — marriage, criminal — with a clear map of exactly what was and wasn't searched.
3
You get a dated badge. One link, in minutes. It expires, because a check is a snapshot and pretending otherwise would be dishonest.
Nevertells you someone is "safe." That's your call, not an app's.
Neverstores or exposes anything about you. You stay invisible.
Neverhides gaps. If a state wasn't searched, you'll see it.
Alwayswith his consent. He chooses to be verified — honesty, offered.
